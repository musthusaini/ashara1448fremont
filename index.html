Welcome

Ashara Mubaraka 1448H

Fremont Jamaat warmly welcomes all Mumineen to join the Ashara Mubaraka relay at Masjid e Mohammedi. We are honoured to host the live relay of the sacred waaz of our beloved Dai al-Mutlaq.

🕌

Venue

Masjid e Mohammedi, 1121 Old Canyon Road, Fremont, CA 94536. The masjid serves as the primary relay centre for the Bay Area community during Ashara Mubaraka.

📡

Live Relay

High-quality live video and audio relay of Ashara Mubaraka waaz will be broadcast at the masjid daily throughout the ten sacred days. Full facilities will be available.

✈️

Getting Here

Three major international airports serve the Fremont area — San Jose (SJC), Oakland (OAK), and San Francisco (SFO) — all within 23 miles of the masjid.

🤝

Hospitality

Fremont Jamaat will provide full hospitality arrangements for visiting Mumineen. For accommodation assistance and any other queries, please contact the jamaat office.
